# Android Home Screen Widget

The Android home screen widget shows your tasks at a glance and lets you mark them done without opening the app.

## Adding the Widget

Long-press an empty area of your home screen, select **Widgets**, find **Super Productivity**, and place the widget. The widget is resizable—drag its edges to show more or fewer tasks.

## What the Widget Shows

By default the widget shows your **Today** tasks: tasks that are scheduled for today or have a due date of today, in the same order as the Today View in the app.

Each row shows the task title. Completed tasks appear with a strikethrough and are dimmed.

## Marking a Task Done

Tap the checkbox on the left of any task row to toggle it done. The widget updates immediately with an optimistic state, then syncs with the app in the background.

If the app is open, the change is applied live. If the app is closed, the change is queued and applied the next time the app starts.

## Opening a Task in the App

Tap the **task title** to open the app and jump directly to that task.

Tap the **widget header** (the title bar) to open the app without navigating to a specific task.

## Switching the Active List

By default the widget shows Today tasks. You can switch it to a **project** or **tag** instead:

1. Tap the list icon in the widget header.
2. Select any project, tag, or **Today's Tasks** from the picker.
3. The widget updates immediately.

The selected list is shown in the widget header. When a project or tag is active and the **Show all-time tasks** toggle is off, the header shows **(Today)** to indicate only today-scoped tasks are visible.

## Showing All-Time vs. Today-Only Tasks

When a project or tag is selected, the context picker includes a **Show all-time tasks** toggle:

- **Off (default):** only tasks in that project or tag that are also scheduled for today are shown.
- **On:** all tasks in that project or tag are shown regardless of due date.

This toggle has no effect when **Today's Tasks** is selected—that view is always today-only.

## Hiding Completed Tasks

Tap the eye icon in the widget header to hide or show completed tasks. The setting persists across widget refreshes.

## Sync and the Widget

The widget reads data written by the app. After changes in the app (adding tasks, rescheduling, etc.) the widget refreshes automatically when the app syncs its data to the widget store.

If the widget looks stale, open the app briefly to trigger a data refresh.
